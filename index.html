🎲 Today's Wildcard
The Study That Built the Mediterranean Diet Category — and What Almost Killed It
In 2013, the PREDIMED study was published in the New England Journal of Medicine and immediately became the most-cited nutritional science paper in a decade. It showed a 30% reduction in cardiovascular events on a Mediterranean diet vs. a low-fat control. CAVA's founders cited this research directly in early investor materials. The entire Mediterranean fast-casual category owes part of its credibility to PREDIMED.

In 2018, the paper was retracted — methodological issues with randomization. It was republished months later with corrected analysis. The corrected version still showed significant cardiovascular benefits. The category survived its biggest potential scientific crisis with almost no public awareness that it had happened at all.

This is the intellectual origin story of the billion-dollar category you operate in. And almost no one in the F&B industry knows the retraction story.
💡 Why this for you today: CAVA just raised full-year guidance. Mediterranean is a capital magnet. But the category's scientific foundation almost collapsed in 2018 and quietly held. Understanding that story makes you a more sophisticated operator — and a more compelling one when talking to investors who've done the homework.
