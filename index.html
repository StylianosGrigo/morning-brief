The Unsexy Density Argument No One Is Pricing In

The stat: Greece ranks 2nd globally in restaurants per capita — approximately 2,100 restaurants per 100,000 residents, behind only Cyprus. Total addressable market: roughly 90,000 restaurant units in Greece. Of those, 87% are single-location, owner-operated businesses with essentially zero tech stack.

What this means: The Greek restaurant market isn't a niche. It's a density play that rivals Manhattan in unit count, and it's almost entirely un-digitized. The UK has a similar population but half the restaurant count — UK restaurant tech investment is 10× Greece's. The gap is structural, not cultural.

The reframe: A tool that charges €29/month and captures just 1% penetration of Greek restaurants is a €26,100 MRR business. At 5% it's €130K MRR. The TAM argument for STIQ's Greek market focus is far stronger than it looks on the surface — it just doesn't come with VC-friendly pitch deck comparables because no one's counting the tavernas.
