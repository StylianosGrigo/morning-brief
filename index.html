Spyros Skouras — from Greek-American diner busboy to 20th Century Fox CEO (1942-1962)

Born in 1893 in a small village in the Peloponnese, Skouras emigrated to St. Louis at 17 and within a decade ran a chain of Greek-American diners with his two brothers. He pivoted into theaters in the 1920s, eventually taking over 20th Century Fox at the brink of bankruptcy in 1942 and running it for 20 years — through CinemaScope, the Marilyn Monroe era, and Cleopatra's near-implosion.
