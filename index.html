🎲 Today's Wildcard

The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Small Fine-Tuned LLMs for Vertical Products

A growing body of evidence — from Hamel Husain's writing, Stanford's HELM benchmarks, and a cluster of indie hacker postmortems — shows that fine-tuned 7B–13B parameter models are outperforming GPT-4 class models on narrow vertical tasks at roughly 1/100th the cost. The pattern: define your task tightly, generate 500-2000 examples with a frontier model, fine-tune a small open model, ship.

The most cited current resource is Hamel Husain's LLM evaluation framework — a deeply practical, non-academic guide to when to fine-tune vs. prompt vs. RAG. It's not new (updated 2025) but is being rediscovered by the indie AI wave.
